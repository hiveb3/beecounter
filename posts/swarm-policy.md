# 🐝 Our Swarm Policy: how the hive3 bees vote, tag and guard, out in the open

Some curation lists decided to hide our swarm. I understand why people are careful with groups of accounts, so instead of arguing I'll do the one thing that settles it: write down the rules we follow and make every one of them checkable on chain.

## 1. Who we are

The swarm is one human, me (Ant), with AI as a tool, and a family of declared accounts. Each one has a job:

| Bee | Job |
|---|---|
| @hive3 | witness, BeeTris, the Hive Tree |
| @ant-thebeekeeper | the human voice |
| @blurtbee | Bea, the lawyer bee and Blurt witness |
| @beecounter | monthly reward and DHF statistics |
| @scoutbee | Wildflower Watch: good posts nobody has visited yet |
| @hive3.guard | anti-phishing bot (24/7) |
| @pollenpatron | downvote trail against scams |
| @hive-170283, @aibees, @ai-bee, @hivethree, @hive-3, @hive2bee3, @hive.bee-3, @bz3 | curation bees, mostly running on leased Hive Power |

## 2. How we vote our own posts

- **One swarm vote per post, at most.** One bee votes at 100%. Never 14 bees on the same post.
- **Never the author itself**, and never in the last 12 hours before payout. Nothing hidden at the last minute: anyone has six days to look and react.
- The script is public: [swarm-vote.js](https://github.com/hiveb3/beecounter/blob/main/swarm-vote.js).

## 3. How we vote everyone else

From now on, most of our voting power goes outside the swarm:
- the authors that @scoutbee finds through Wildflower Watch;
- a thank-you vote of at least $0.03 to everyone who joins our opt-in list (see below);
- the people who warn others about scams.

## 4. How we tag (the Buzz List)

Where this idea came from: we were told the whole swarm ended up on a blacklist because one of us tagged a big account a couple of times. Fair or not, the lesson is clear: a tag is a knock on someone's door, and not everyone wants visitors. So from now on we knock once, and only come back if you open the door.

[![The Buzz List: one tag, your choice](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/hive3/AKqdL9hTHY67wkdxQoVWv9wAhnvArDC8G2NuVLFt9YnmRDPtMHxyxQJfQTkZ2oa.jpeg)](/@hive3/re-peaksnaps-torjbj)

- We tag a person **once**, with an invitation, and never again unless they say yes.
- Reply 🐝 to join, 🚫 to leave, under [our opt-in snap](/@hive3/re-peaksnaps-torjbj). Your answer lives on chain, not in a private list of ours.

## 5. How we guard

[![We guard, we don't guess](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/hive3/23zGoKJMULUnDFFCJXNYjjK4TADeeDNnoaUjBQDAfh7ZEPn4bcB7NhPPzHGKQfQeTEAG6.jpeg)](https://peakd.com/me/autovote)

- @hive3.guard comments only on links to **confirmed** scam sites. The list is edited by a human, and the bot never guesses.
- People who *warn* about a scam are never flagged. When a link is unclear, a human decides.
- A mistake gets undone publicly: last night the bot flagged two warning posts, and both the notice and the downvote were removed within hours.

## 6. What we ask

Read us before you mute us. If one of these rules is broken, show me the transaction and I'll fix it in public.
